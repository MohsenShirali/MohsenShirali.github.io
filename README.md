# Mohsen Shirali

**Postdoctoral Researcher — Faculty of Economics and Business (FEB), KU Leuven, Belgium**

I received my Ph.D. (2025) and M.Sc. (2013) in Computer Engineering from Shahid Beheshti University (SBU), Tehran, Iran. My research focuses on the Internet of Things (IoT), data analytics, process mining, Artificial Intelligence (AI), security and privacy. Since August 2026, I have been a Postdoctoral Researcher at the Faculty of Economics and Business (FEB), KU Leuven, Belgium. From July 2024 to July 2026, I worked as a Postdoctoral Researcher at ICTEAM, UCLouvain, where my research focused on anomaly detection, time-series analysis, system modelling, and digital twins for Cyber-Physical Systems. I previously held research positions at KU Leuven (2023–2024) and the University of Passau (2019–2020), and I have lectured at SBU and Ahvaz Sama University. In addition to academia, I have several years of R&D experience in industry. I have received scholarships from DAAD and Iran’s MSRT and have published in journals and conferences.
